The annual trend model showed a positive direction, although the p-value of 0.0802 indicates that the observed trend should be interpreted with caution rather than treated as statistically conclusive. Fraud & Risk Analytics
Digital Payment Fraud Analysis & Risk Insight Team Leader · Mock Project · Bitlabs Academy

Overview

Client: A digital payment fintech platform processing millions of transactions between buyers and sellers.

Business Problem: An increase in potentially fraudulent transactions was creating financial risk and affecting customer trust.

Objective: Analyze transaction and user data to identify potential fraud indicators, suspicious relationships, and recurring behavioral patterns that could support fraud monitoring and risk management.

Project Approach

Data Understanding & Preparation
Reviewed transaction and user data to understand the structure, key variables, and fraud related indicators.

Data Cleaning & Quality Checks
Reviewed and prepared multiple datasets to ensure data quality and analytical readiness. The process included:

  • Initial data profiling and statistical checks
  • Duplicate and unique value checks
  • Missing value analysis and treatment
  • Data type conversion and standardization
  • Data consistency checks across related datasets
  • Validation of promotion related transaction data
  • Duplicate record identification and handling
  • Outlier detection and investigation
  • Risk based transaction screening using percentile thresholds
  • Final validation and export of cleaned datasets

The cleaning process also included investigating potentially suspicious transaction characteristics based on transaction amount, buyer and seller activity, payment methods, payment providers, and buyer seller combinations.

Feature Engineering
Created analytical features to identify unusual transaction behavior, suspicious buyer seller relationships, and potential promotion exploitation.

Buyer Seller Relationship Score

  • Calculated transaction frequency for each buyer seller pair.
  • Used the 98th percentile as a threshold to identify unusually frequent relationships.
  • Assigned a relationship score to highlight higher risk buyer seller pairs.
  • Analyzed high scoring relationships for further investigation.

Transaction Frequency Metrics

  • Calculated time intervals between transactions.
  • Identified potential activity bursts based on unusually short transaction intervals.
  • Flagged unusually large gaps between transactions as potential changes in behavioral patterns.

Promotion Exploitation Indicator

  • Measured promotion usage frequency by buyer.
  • Identified unusually frequent promotion usage as a potential exploitation signal.
  • Identified promotions with the highest usage among potentially exploitative users.
  • Examined the relationship between promotion exploitation and transaction value.
  • Measured the contribution of potentially exploitative buyers to total transaction value.

Scaling & Normalization
Normalized transaction amounts to support comparative analysis, and normalized time based metrics to make behavioral patterns more comparable across observations.

Advanced SQL Analysis
Developed SQL views and stored procedures to support recurring fraud monitoring.

  • Suspicious Buyer Seller Pairs: a reporting view summarizing potentially suspicious relationships based on transaction frequency and transaction value.
  • Flagged User Transactions: a view to identify transactions involving flagged or blacklisted users.
  • Monthly Fraud Reporting: a stored procedure structure for generating recurring fraud reports containing fraud volume, flagged users, and suspicious buyer seller relationships.
  • Automated Promotion Abuse Detection: a procedure concept to identify and record potential promotion abuse cases.

Behavioral & Cohort Analysis
Analyzed buyer behavior over time to identify recurring activity patterns, retention trends, and potential risk signals.

Cohort Analysis

  • Grouped buyers based on their first transaction month.
  • Tracked transaction activity across subsequent months.
  • Calculated buyer retention rates by cohort.
  • Visualized cohort retention using a heatmap.
  • Analyzed Lifetime Value (LTV) across buyer cohorts.

Behavioral Risk Analysis

  • Identified buyers with prolonged periods of inactivity.
  • Analyzed buyers with repeated transactions involving the same seller.
  • Compared recurring behavioral patterns to identify buyers that may require further investigation.

Insight Generation
Analyzed transaction activity around promotional campaign periods to identify potential changes in transaction behavior, including defining campaign periods, measuring daily transaction volume, identifying transaction spikes, and analyzing cashback amounts per transaction. These analyses were used to assess whether unusual transaction activity or cashback patterns emerged during promotional periods and could warrant further investigation.

Project Output

  • SQL Monitoring & Reporting: helps the client automate fraud monitoring, prioritize suspicious buyer seller relationships and flagged activity, and generate reports for the period being reviewed.
  • Buyer Seller Relationship Score: helps prioritize unusually frequent buyer seller relationships for further fraud risk investigation.
  • Promotion Exploitation Indicator: helps identify unusual promotion usage and prioritize potential abuse cases for further investigation.
  • Transaction Frequency Metrics: helps identify unusual transaction activity, including rapid transaction bursts and irregular gaps, for further investigation.
  • Retention Matrix Heatmap: helps the client monitor buyer retention patterns and identify cohorts with stronger or weaker engagement over time.
