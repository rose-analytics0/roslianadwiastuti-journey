Structured bookkeeping and financial reporting support for eligible Indonesian micro businesses and sole proprietors with relatively simple operations and transaction records.

This service is designed for businesses that need help organizing transaction records, verifying supporting documents, reconciling basic balances, and preparing financial statements with reference to SAK EMKM (Indonesian Financial Accounting Standards for Micro, Small, and Medium Entities), where applicable to eligible Indonesian entities.

Who This Service Is For

This package is intended for sole proprietors and small businesses with relatively simple operations and bookkeeping needs, such as:

  • Small food stalls or cafés
  • Small retail or grocery stores
  • Simple online shops
  • Resellers
  • Freelancers and independent service providers
  • Small laundry businesses
  • Small barbershops or salons
  • Home based food businesses
  • Individual service businesses
  • Similar businesses with straightforward transactions

General Business Profile

The service is generally suitable for businesses with:

  • A simple management structure where the owner is directly involved in operations
  • One business location
  • Up to one business bank account
  • Cash, bank transfer, or e-wallet transactions
  • Basic or single entry bookkeeping records
  • Transactions primarily recorded in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR)
  • No inter branch or inter entity transactions

Package Limits

  • Reporting period: 1 month
  • Maximum transactions: 100 transactions per month
  • Business: 1 business
  • Location: 1 location
  • Business bank account: Maximum 1 account
  • Inventory: Basic
  • Raw materials: Basic, without complex production costing
  • Accounts receivable/payable: Basic
  • Fixed assets: Basic
  • Currency: Indonesian Rupiah (IDR)
  • Branches / multiple entities: Not included

Note: The 100 transaction limit is a service package limit and is not a regulatory definition of a micro business.

Scope of Services

Bookkeeping & Data Entry

  • Enter transactions from POS/cashier records, e-wallets, bank statements, and manual transaction records
  • Categorize transactions into relevant accounts
  • Organize bookkeeping data in a structured format

Data Verification

  • Check the completeness of submitted data
  • Match records against available receipts, invoices, transfer confirmations, or other supporting documents
  • Identify discrepancies or incomplete information

Reconciliation

  • Reconcile cash balances with bookkeeping records
  • Perform basic reconciliation for one business bank account
  • Identify differences based on the available records

Excel Processing

  • Process and validate transaction data using Microsoft Excel
  • Prepare transaction and cash summaries as part of the bookkeeping process
  • Prepare the worksheets required for financial statement preparation

Financial Statement Preparation

Main outputs: Statement of Financial Position, Income Statement, and Notes to the Financial Statements (CALK).

Applicable Accounting Framework: The financial statements are prepared with reference to SAK EMKM (Indonesian Financial Accounting Standards for Micro, Small, and Medium Entities) for eligible Indonesian entities where applicable. These financial statements help business owners understand their financial position, business performance, and relevant financial information to support business management and decision making.

Financial Statement Overview

A brief explanation of key items and observations, including revenue, expenses, profit or loss, cash position, and key observations from the financial statements.
