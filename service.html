I go beyond simple copy and paste data entry. I use spreadsheet formulas and validation techniques to help identify incorrect, missing, duplicated, or inconsistent information during the process.

For example, I have worked with WhatsApp order messages containing emojis, tags, inconsistent product names, mixed units such as pcs, kg, and dozen, and decimal quantities. I can extract the relevant product and quantity information, standardize the data, flag unclear records for manual checking, and turn the results into a structured table ready for further use.

I also create formula based solutions for data validation and processing, such as checking values against reference lists, identifying missing fields and duplicates, extracting information from text, categorizing data, calculating KPIs, and creating filtered interactive views.
