Get to Know Me

Who I Am

I am an Accounting graduate with professional experience as an External Auditor, complemented by strong hands on experience in data analytics, business analysis, and operational processes.

My experience across different professional environments has given me a practical perspective on how financial information, business processes, operational activities, and data connect to support reliable information, meaningful analysis, and informed decision making.

Professional Journey

Understanding Processes

My professional journey began with 8+ years of freelance experience in production operations, collaborating through a third party liaison on production orders for international clients and handling production volumes of up to approximately 4,000 units per production cycle. I was directly involved in production processes, quality inspection, and documentation. This experience developed my attention to detail, discipline, and understanding of processes from inputs through finished outputs.

Working with Operational Data

My experience then expanded into retail inventory control, where I worked with stock opname, inventory reconciliation, transaction verification, discrepancy investigation, and documentation. This strengthened my understanding of how accurate, consistent, and well structured data supports reliable day to day business operations.

Developing Data Analytics Capability

Through various practical projects, I developed hands on experience in data processing and analysis using Excel, Google Sheets, SQL, Python (Pandas & NumPy), and Tableau. I have worked with datasets including 50,000 transaction records, 119,390 hotel reservation records, and product, price, and transaction data for e-commerce inventory and price tracking, including the development of interactive client views.

My project work has included data cleaning, validation, exploratory analysis, business analysis, visualization, forecasting, risk focused analysis, and spreadsheet based reporting. I also worked with unstructured WhatsApp order messages, transforming mixed messages containing text, emojis, tags, and informal formats into structured tables through product identification, quantity extraction, unit standardization, product code matching, and manual review flagging.

These experiences strengthened my ability to organize unstructured information, work with large datasets, validate data, and turn raw inputs into structured outputs and practical business insights.

Applying These Capabilities in External Audit

As an External Auditor, I brought together my understanding of business processes, documentation, data accuracy, and analytical thinking while working on audit engagements for 11 companies across 6 industries: Construction, Media, Trading, Cooperatives, Services, and Education.

My responsibilities included transaction testing, vouching, document verification, financial reconciliation, audit evidence evaluation, and audit working paper preparation, as well as coordination with audit teams and client representatives. This experience further strengthened my ability to examine information critically, trace data to supporting evidence, identify discrepancies, and understand financial information within its business context.

The Common Thread

I believe accurate, consistent, and reliable data is essential for effective analysis, business operations, and informed decision making.

My Goal

My goal is to combine accounting knowledge, audit experience, operational understanding, and data analytics to help businesses work with more reliable information, clearer processes, and more informed decisions.
