Who I Am

I specialize in data processing and data entry, with a focus on turning raw and unstructured business information into accurate, structured, and ready-to-use data.

My professional journey has taken me through production operations, inventory control, external audit, and data analytics. Working across these different environments has given me a practical understanding of how information is collected, processed, checked, reconciled, and used to support day-to-day business activities and decision making.

Professional Journey
Understanding Processes

My professional journey began with 8+ years of freelance experience in production operations, working through a third-party liaison on production orders for international clients. I worked with production volumes of up to approximately 4,000 units per production cycle and was directly involved in material preparation, sewing, component installation, finishing, packing, quality inspection, and production monitoring.

This experience taught me to work carefully within defined procedures, maintain consistency across high-volume production, identify issues during the process, and pay close attention to quality and client requirements.

Working with Operational Data

My experience later expanded into retail inventory control, where I worked with physical stock counting, inventory reconciliation, transaction verification, discrepancy investigation, and inventory documentation.

I compared physical inventory with recorded quantities, reviewed inventory movements and supporting documentation, traced transactions when discrepancies occurred, and coordinated with other teams to follow up on inventory-related issues. This gave me practical experience working with operational data where accuracy, consistency, and traceability matter.

Developing Data Processing & Analytics Skills

Alongside my professional experience, I developed my data processing and analytics skills through project-based learning and practical assignments.

I learned and practiced working with Excel, Google Sheets, SQL, Python, Pandas, NumPy, Tableau, and related tools across different types of business and analytical problems. My work included data cleaning and standardization, missing value and duplicate checks, data validation, spreadsheet analysis, SQL querying, exploratory data analysis, visualization, dashboard development, forecasting, clustering, and business interpretation.

I worked with datasets including 50,000 digital payment transaction records and 119,390 hotel reservation records, as well as e-commerce product, inventory, transaction, and pricing data. I also practiced transforming unstructured WhatsApp order messages containing informal text, emojis, inconsistent product names, and mixed units into structured order data using extraction, standardization, product matching, validation, and manual review.

Through these projects, I learned how to move from raw information → data preparation → validation → analysis → structured output → business insight.

Applying Critical Review in External Audit

I then worked as an External Auditor, where I worked on audit engagements involving 11 companies across six industries: Construction, Media, Trading, Cooperatives, Services, and Education.

My responsibilities included transaction testing, vouching, document verification, reconciliation, audit evidence evaluation, and preparation of audit working papers. I compared accounting records with bank statements, ledgers, disclosures, third-party confirmations, and supporting documents, while following up on identified differences and missing information.

This experience strengthened my ability to check information against supporting evidence, identify discrepancies, trace information to its source, and understand data within its business context.

The Common Thread

Looking across these experiences, I found a common thread in the way I work with information:

check → compare → verify → identify discrepancies → organize → make the data usable

Whether working with production output, inventory records, financial information, or data from practical projects, I have consistently worked with information that needs to be checked, organized, and understood before it can be reliably used.

Current Focus

Today, I am focusing my professional direction on Data Processing, Data Entry, Data Extraction, and Data Management.

I bring together what I have gained from production operations, inventory control, external audit, accounting, and project-based data analytics learning to work with raw and unstructured information.

My current focus is on extracting, cleaning, organizing, validating, standardizing, and processing data so that it becomes accurate, structured, traceable, and ready for operational use, reporting, or further analysis.
