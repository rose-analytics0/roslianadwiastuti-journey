Who I Am

Who I Am

I am an Accounting graduate and External Auditor with cross industry professional experience, complemented by strong hands on experience in data analytics, business analysis, and operational processes.

My experience has developed across different environments, giving me a practical perspective on how financial information, business processes, operational activities, and data connect to support reliable information and better decision making.

Professional Journey

Understanding Processes

My professional journey began in production operations and quality control, where I worked with SOP based processes, material preparation, production stages, quality inspections, defect identification, and high volume production. This experience developed my discipline, attention to detail, and ability to monitor processes from inputs through finished outputs.

Working with Operational Data

I later gained experience in retail operations and inventory control, where I worked with stock opname, inventory reconciliation, transaction verification, inventory records, and operational documentation. This strengthened my understanding of how reliable records and traceable processes support day to day business operations.

Developing Data Analytics Capability

My interest in data analytics led me to develop strong hands on experience with Excel, SQL, Python, Pandas, and Tableau through practical projects. I worked on data cleaning, validation, exploratory analysis, business analysis, visualization, forecasting, and risk focused analytics. These projects also allowed me to work with large datasets and translate analytical results into structured outputs, visualizations, and practical business insights.

Applying These Capabilities in External Audit

As an External Auditor, I brought together my understanding of business processes, documentation, data accuracy, and analytical thinking. I worked across different industries and performed transaction testing, vouching, document verification, financial data reconciliation, audit evidence evaluation, client and audit team coordination, and audit working paper preparation.

The Common Thread

Across these experiences, I have developed a consistent way of working: understand the process, examine the information carefully, identify patterns or inconsistencies, and turn findings into structured and useful outputs.
