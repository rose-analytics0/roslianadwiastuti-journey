Get to Know Me

Who I Am

I am an Accounting graduate and External Auditor with cross industry professional experience, complemented by strong hands on experience in data analytics, business analysis, and operational processes.

My experience has developed across different environments, giving me a practical perspective on how financial information, business processes, operational activities, and data connect to support reliable information and better decision making.

Professional Journey

Understanding Processes

My professional journey began with 8+ years of freelance experience in production operations for projects serving international clients, handling production volumes of up to approximately 4,000 units per production cycle. I was directly involved in production processes, quality inspection, and documentation. This experience developed my attention to detail, discipline, and understanding of processes from inputs through finished outputs.

Working with Operational Data

My experience then expanded into retail inventory control, where I worked with stock opname, inventory reconciliation, transaction verification, discrepancy investigation, and documentation. This strengthened my understanding of how accurate, consistent, and well structured data supports reliable day-to-day business operations.

Developing Data Analytics Capability

Through various practical projects, I developed hands-on experience in data processing and analysis using Excel, Google Sheets, SQL, Python (Pandas & NumPy), and Tableau. I have worked with datasets including 50,000 transaction records, 119,390 hotel reservation records, and product, price, and transaction data for e-commerce inventory and price tracking, including the development of interactive client views. My project work has covered data cleaning, validation, exploratory analysis, business analysis, visualization, forecasting, risk focused analysis, and spreadsheet based reporting. These experiences strengthened my ability to work with large datasets and turn data into structured outputs, visualizations, and practical business insights.

Applying These Capabilities in External Audit

As an External Auditor, I brought together my understanding of business processes, documentation, data accuracy, and analytical thinking while working on audit engagements for 11 companies across 6 industries: Construction, Media, Trading, Cooperatives, Services, and Education. My responsibilities included transaction testing, vouching, document verification, financial reconciliation, audit evidence evaluation, and audit working paper preparation, as well as coordination with audit teams and client representatives. This experience further strengthened my ability to examine information critically, trace data to supporting evidence, identify discrepancies, and understand financial information within its business context.

The Common Thread

Across these experiences, I have had the opportunity to work with different types of data, information, and business processes. I believe data is one of the important foundations for business growth and decision making. For that reason, maintaining data that is accurate, consistent, structured, and reliable is not simply an administrative task. It is part of maintaining the quality of information that businesses rely on to operate, analyze performance, manage risk, and make informed decisions.
